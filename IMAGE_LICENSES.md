## User Uploaded Photographic References

Photographs uploaded by end users during Step 1 of the evaluation wizard are handled under the following constraints:
* **Storage:** Encoded strictly as transient base64 data URLs in local session storage and embedded onto the generated PDF report.
* **Non-Analytical Purpose:** Clearly marked on reports with the disclosure:  
  > *"Visual reference only. Consumer smartphone photography cannot determine natural origin, microscopic inclusion relief, or quantitative spectral absorption."*
